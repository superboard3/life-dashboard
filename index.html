Nutrition 101

What is a Calorie?

A calorie is just a unit of energy. Food gives us energy, and our bodies burn energy to stay alive and move.

What is a Caloric Deficit?

To lose fat, you need to eat slightly fewer calories than your body burns every day. This forces your body to use stored fat for the missing energy.

How Many Meals?

Meal timing doesn't matter as much as total calories. Do what fits your schedule! Most women do well with:
• 3 moderately sized meals.
• OR 3 smaller meals + 1 or 2 snacks to keep energy up.

Simple Meal Ideas

Easy, accessible meals (No beans, no quinoa, no stress).

Breakfast Ideas

  • Scrambled eggs with 2 slices of homemade bread/toast and a piece of fruit.
  • Greek yogurt topped with berries and a drizzle of organic honey.
  • Oatmeal made with milk, swirled with a spoon of peanut butter or Nutella.

Snack Ideas

  • Apple slices dipped in peanut butter.
  • Rice cakes spread with Smucker's Jam or Nutella.
  • A piece of string cheese and a handful of crackers.
  • Deli turkey slices rolled up with a little cheese.
